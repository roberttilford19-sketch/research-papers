## Errors and anomalies

The document's central anomaly is methodological and is stated in its own prompts: the analysis was instructed to assume the text true, to derive facts from it, and to build on them without scepticism. Conclusions produced that way are presented in the generated text with the vocabulary of fact and law, but they rest entirely on the initial assumption. Specific anomalies follow from that. The closing case study reports that neutralising a plague-era possession cycle diminishes modern "plague-like" possession clusters, instanced by opioid crises, by forty percent; no source, data, or measurement is given, and the figure is generated, not found. Named laws — of dynamic allocation, ethical engagement, and earthly-spiritual reciprocity — are the analysis tool's coinages, although the text's layout lets them read as the book's own teaching. Case details about Louise, Dr. Jastram, and others appear inside generated examples and cannot be separated, from this document alone, into book case history and generated illustration. The same analysis sections recur in near-identical form more than once, so the document's length substantially overstates its distinct content.
