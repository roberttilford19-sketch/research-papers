## Abstract

This Federal Bureau of Investigation electronic communication, dated October 2024, documents contact with a witness who reported repeated observations of unidentified objects appearing as plasma orbs near the witness's residence. According to the communication, the witness had been referred to the Bureau by a Bureau liaison contact in August 2024, and in October 2024 provided multiple video clips showing objects near the residence, including renewed activity captured on the evenings of two October dates. The communication records that the witness had privately documented observations of anomalous lights appearing as orbs dating back to a first observation in the early morning hours of November 2021, observed near the property and sometimes as close as 200 yards, hovering, moving, above the horizon, close to the ground, within trees, and in or near water. It further records the witness's efforts to collect data with trail cameras and other sensors, and the witness's report of spikes in gamma radiation correlated with observation times and of unusual effects on electronics and global positioning data. It notes that the witness described having had no prior interest in the subject and a skeptical outlook, had lived at the residence since 2006 without earlier anomalous observations, wished to protect the family's privacy, and did not want the information to become public.
