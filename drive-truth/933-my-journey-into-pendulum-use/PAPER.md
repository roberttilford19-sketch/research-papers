## Finished Text

My introduction to dowsing began many years ago, when my cousin, Albert, demonstrated the art of “water witching”. He  used two L shaped wire hangers that he held in both hands, equal distance apart, about waist high …These rods acted like omnidirectional finders. 

He indicated, among other things, that they pinpointed the direction of buried water lines underground…showing how they crossed over the location of an underground water pipe on his property. He then demonstrated how the rods  pointed right or left to indicate the direction and flow of the water. He said he could use the method to find the best location of water wells on someone's property. 

I dismissed it at the time, assuming he already knew the location & directions of the properties water line and was playing a trick on me. With that said I couldn't see how he was manipulating the rods to move without any visible motion from his wrists?

I held the L shaped hangers in my hands, at about waist level and they didn't move for me. I also wasn't feeling it. I assumed I didn't have the ability or whatever. 

Years later, I watched my uncle trace a buried water line leading away from a main to an adjacent house in Kansas using bent wire metal hangers. Sure enough he did it and found the pipe. 

Again I rationalized it away…because he was simply following a straight line along the connection point. This didn’t explain how he pinpointed the pipe’s abrupt right hand turn underneath a concrete slab and into the kitchen.  

Years later, I studied references to dowsing in declassified CIA files from Project Stargate which piqued my interest in the subject once again.  

If the government —took dowsing seriously, perhaps there was some merit to it. I thought I had better reexamine the subject once again more carefully. 

I ordered a pair of copper dowsing rods from Walmart, but their design and length made them awkward for me to use because they had to be held wide enough apart as the rods could freely spin around and they would bump my arms or biceps. Regardless I experimented with them and found they would work…for example if I held them steady enough and said “point to the window”...they would eerily point toward the window despite my facing to the left of the window or the right of the window…how the hell did it do this.

I would ask the rods to “point in the direction of the door of my apartment” …and the rods would slowly turn in that direction. 

I really couldn't explain how this was happening but I  was assured that I wasn't consciously trying to move the rods in any direction?  

While intrigued I grew tired of the rods…

While putting the rods away in the case that came with it, a rose quartz pendulum in a small plastic bag  fell out on the floor. It was then I remembered the dowsing rod kit also included a pendulum. 

The included instructions explained not only the use of the rods but the use of the pendulum as well so…

Skeptical yet curious, I tested the pendulum and experimented with it numerous times.

To my surprise, the pendulum moved independently of my conscious intent, much like the rods…but it was easier for me to use a pendulum, because of the size and that I could use it with one hand and carry it around in my pocket. 

My yes response was clockwise and my no response was counterclockwise.  Again I was not consciously moving the pendulum or willing it to spin this way or that. This I was assured. 

Since then, I’ve honed my skills with the pendulum in particular, studying various techniques through YouTube videos and books by authors like Raymond Grace, Dan Baldwin, and Emily Anderson. 

I gradually expanded my application of the pendulum beyond simple yes and no queries—incorporating such things as cleansing energy in a room, and even remote healing. 

Whatever was happening here I couldn't explain…

Further research indicated something called an “ideomotor response”, meaning subtle involuntary micro muscle movements. That my unconscious mind was moving the pendulum without my conscious control. This was very bizarre to me. 

I can tell you this though when the rods and or pendulum move it's the closest thing to magic I ever experienced. 

Ultimately, I concluded that something more had to be going on here…it was almost  like an invisible hand within my hand was moving the pendulum despite my staring and concentrating on keeping my physical hand perfectly still. Other times it appeared I may be influencing the pendulum swing, but not consciously willing it to go this way or that. If that makes any sense. 


My journey using the pendulum transformed my skepticism into a deeper appreciation for dowsing in general.
