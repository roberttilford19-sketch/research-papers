## Premise

The document sets out a complete method for exorcism at a distance and argues for its design. The method has two objects, not one: to remove a possessing entity from a host, and to settle what happens to the entity afterwards, so that it does not simply move on to another host. It proceeds by pendulum diagnosis, by an alpha state of relaxed attention, and by spiritual assistance — a guide, angel, or guardian angel acting as proxy — so that the practitioner never confronts the entity directly. Its governing conviction is that there are two victims in a possession, the host and the possessing entity itself, and that compassion and forgiveness, following the example of Jesus forgiving his torturers, are the means by which the cycle of harm is broken rather than merely interrupted.

## Abstract

This document states and then elaborates a methodology for distance exorcism. Its steps, in their final integrated form, are these: identify whether a person is possessed, voluntarily or involuntarily; obtain permission from the person's higher self; confirm the possession and identify the number and type of entities by pendulum dowsing; enter an alpha state through relaxation and deep breathing; avoid all direct confrontation, working only from a safe distance; call on spiritual volunteers — guides, angels, archangels, or guardian angels — who can act only when invoked by a living soul; have those proxies confront the entity with a choice between transitioning willingly to a higher spiritual dimension in the company of a trusted, loving figure, or containment in an uncomfortable small black box with nails sticking into it, under a five-second countdown; complete the transition; and verify by pendulum that the possession is resolved. The elaborations argue that this design protects the practitioner, because the confrontation is delegated and the work is done remotely; that it breaks the cycle of possession, because the entity is relocated rather than cast loose; and that it addresses the gravest harms the document attributes to possessing entities, including driving hosts to violent acts and to suicide, by treating the entity itself as a traumatised victim to be healed.
