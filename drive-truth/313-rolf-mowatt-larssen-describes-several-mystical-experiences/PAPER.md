## Abstract

This document is an extensive set of compiled notes summarizing a video interview of more than four hours with Rolf Mowatt-Larssen. Its center is his account of a 1991 pilgrimage to Mount Athos, Greece, during which, while napping, he experienced time evaporating as he moved backward into the fifteenth century, living for months within the experience as a monk he calls Odius Maximus — eating, walking, socializing, and sleeping — while aware that he was dreaming and uncertain he could return. Within that experience he reports three dreams within the dream, including a room of numbered people and a round room with seven doors. The notes also record his earlier dreams that he says came true, a 1982 dream of nuclear war in 2030–2034, a dream he connects to the assassination of Anwar Sadat, and encounters with a figure he later identified as the Virgin Mary, culminating in a waking apparition in a Paris hotel room in 1998 that the notes present as delivering non-verbal messages, among them "deliver the message," "write what you see," and "don't strive." Later passes gather his conclusions: that heaven and hell are in the mind, that a timeless universe at rest underlies ours, that history is not inevitable, that the self must be shed, and that his prophetic dreams made him, in his framing, a watchman obliged to deliver warnings.
