## Abstract

This Federal Bureau of Investigation electronic communication, dated July 2024, records a forensic sketch interview conducted on 11 July 2024 at 26 Federal Plaza in New York with a former United States Army intelligence officer who reported observing an unidentified aerial phenomenon over the Cheyenne Mountains in Colorado. According to the communication, the witness and four other members of his unit observed the object as they exited their office building at 11:25 a.m. on a clear day of about 50 degrees Fahrenheit. The object is described as potato-shaped with distinct edges, painted in a creamy, whitish, opalescent color, somewhat translucent with a slight shimmer. Its surface is described as made up of articulating panels or scales — non-symmetrical, non-overlapping, and irregular — which individually shifted in slow waves while the object itself remained perfectly still. The communication states that after approximately two minutes the object vanished, and it records the witness's specific statement that the object cloaked in the time it took to turn a head, leaving no shadow. It closes by noting that the witness's information and an initial sketch draft were enclosed, and that further drafts would pass between the forensic artist and the witness through the case agent until a final sketch was complete.
