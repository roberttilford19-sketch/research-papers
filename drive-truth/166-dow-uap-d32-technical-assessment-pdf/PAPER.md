## Abstract

This report presents a forensic deconstruction of a mission report, referenced as DOW-UAP-D32, concerning an MQ-9 Reaper asset operating over Syria on 20–21 October 2024. The extracted data record a total flight time of twenty hours and twenty-four minutes, a forty-five-minute contact window from 1559Z to 1644Z, a friendly altitude of 20,088 feet at 144 knots, a primary description of the object as a misshapen and uneven ball of white light, a physical-state classification of plasma, and a sensor package identified as SANTA FE. The report identifies three contradictions. First, the term plasma is technically specific and, in measurement and signature intelligence, would ordinarily rest on spectral or electromagnetic evidence, yet the narrative offers only a visual description. Second, a halo effect and multiple glares suggest radiant intensity beyond the sensor's dynamic range, which sits uneasily beside a benign classification with no mission impact. Third, natural plasma is unstable, yet the object is described as steady over forty-five minutes. The conclusions assign confidence tiers to a high-energy signature, a non-standard collection posture, a persistent presence, and an artificial origin, and the closing implications raise possible geological context, data sanitization between internal and public descriptions, and propulsion that does not rely on conventional aerodynamic surfaces. All findings are the document's own analysis of a source the reader cannot inspect here.
