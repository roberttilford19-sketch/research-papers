## Premise

The form records a Federal Bureau of Investigation interview, conducted in person in the United States, with the second observer in the northeastern orb sighting, in order to preserve that observer's independent account of the same event recorded at census 135. ## Abstract

This Federal Bureau of Investigation FD-302 records an in-person interview with a witness who reported observing balls of light at a residence in the northeastern United States. According to the account recorded in the form, the witness arrived home in the evening at around 9:15 p.m. and noticed a ball of light through the windshield that was yellow and about the size of a basketball. On exiting the vehicle, the witness saw a second ball of light, larger, about the size of a beach ball; the objects were behind a row of trees, since removed, and in front of a further section of trees between the property and a neighbor's property, at an agent-estimated distance of approximately 30 yards and a height of 20 to 30 feet above the ground. The witness stated there had been no health issues or concerns since seeing the objects. A second person came outside, and both observed the objects move laterally to their left, passing behind a tree about 36 yards away and continuing behind a neighbor's house and beyond a tree about 75 yards away, at which point one orb appeared to merge into the other before disappearing abruptly. The form also records the witness's later observation of several white orbs travelling over the house from west to east at higher altitude, and a 1987 observation of a red light circling near power lines, which the witness said had sustained a long-standing interest in the subject.

## Content Account

The form records that the witness was interviewed in person, together with the second observer, at a location redacted in the text. The account begins with arrival home in the evening at around 9:15 p.m. Through the windshield the witness noticed a ball of light that was yellow and about the size of a basketball. Upon exiting the vehicle, the witness saw a second ball of light that was larger, about the size of a beach ball. The objects were behind a row of trees, which have since been taken down, and in front of a section of trees between the witness's property and a neighbor's property behind the home. The witness was able to look at the lights without strain or discomfort, and stated that there have been no health issues or concerns since seeing them. An agent note records that, based on where the witness was standing, the objects were approximately 30 yards away and 20 to 30 feet above the ground.

Upon seeing the lights, the second person came outside to view the objects as well. Both observed the objects move laterally to their left, passing behind a tree approximately 36 yards away at the same height, continuing behind a neighbor's house and beyond a tree approximately 75 yards away. At that point, the form records, one orb merged into the other before disappearing abruptly — the sentence is partially garbled in the extraction but its sense is recoverable from context and from the parallel account at census 135.

The form then records that a few weeks after the event the witness saw several white orbs travel over the house from west to east, at much higher altitude than the earlier orbs. It further records a 1987 episode: the witness saw something strange near power lines, a red light circling around itself at night, described as like the front end of KITT from the television show Knight Rider; the object hovered and then landed near the power station, and the witness stated that since then there has always been an interest in the topic of unidentified anomalous phenomena. Photographs taken by agents with the witness's permission are recorded as attached.

## Errors and Anomalies

On substance, this account differs from the companion account at census 135 in the reported colors and sizes of the first two objects — yellow, basketball-size and beach-ball-size here, against a red sphere of about one meter with a white core there — while agreeing on the event structure. The difference is recorded here as an inter-record discrepancy, not resolved. The 1987 account is a recollection recorded decades later and is attributed to the witness.
