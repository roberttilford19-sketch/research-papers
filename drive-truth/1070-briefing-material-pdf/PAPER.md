## Abstract

The slides define the vocabulary of the STAR GATE programme in the programme's own terms. Psychoenergetics is defined as a mental process by which an individual perceives, communicates with, and/or perturbs characteristics of a designated target, person, or event remote in space and/or time from that individual. Psychokinesis is defined as physical actions performed by mental powers that cannot be explained by known physical means, and extrasensory perception and telepathy as perceptions which cannot be explained by known sensory means. Remote viewing is defined as the acquisition and description, by mental means, of information blocked from ordinary perception by distance, shield, or time, and a remote viewer as a person who perceives, communicates with, and/or perturbs such characteristics. The slides then set out the unit's staffing, its three primary methodologies of extended, coordinate, and written remote viewing, with the six stages of coordinate remote viewing, its secondary methodologies, its categories of tasking and of targets, and the standard project and report forms, closing with the guidance that remote viewers can be used as collectors in conjunction with other intelligence sources throughout the Department of Defense intelligence community and that remote viewing information should not stand alone.

## Content Account

### Definitions

The definitions are the substance of the briefing, and they are reproduced in the abstract above in the slides' own wording because the briefing's purpose was to fix that wording. Two features are worth noting. The definition of psychoenergetics is deliberately broad, covering perceiving, communicating with, and perturbing within a single term, so that information gathering and physical influence are presented as aspects of one process rather than as separate phenomena. And the definition of a remote viewer is broader than the definition of remote viewing: a viewer is defined by perceiving, communicating with, and/or perturbing, whereas remote viewing itself is defined only as the acquisition and description of information.

### Organisation and methodologies

One slide gives the unit's composition as a unit manager, a senior intelligence officer, three operational remote viewers, an intelligence technician, and a secretary. The primary methodologies are three. Extended remote viewing is characterised by physical relaxation, reduction of physical arousal, increase of internal feelings and images, right-brain or hemispheric functioning, and achieving an altered view of reality. Coordinate remote viewing is characterised negatively, by what it minimises: imaginative overlays, environmental overlays, and monitor overlays. Its six stages are listed as stage one, islands, mountains, and deserts; stage two, sites of quality sensory value; stage three, dimensional characteristics; stage four, qualitative mental perceptions; stage five, interrogation of qualitative mental perceptions; and stage six, three-dimensional elements. Written remote viewing is described as phonetically and verbally oriented, and as operating in predictive and search modes. The secondary methodologies are dowsing, psychometry, rendered in the recognised text as "Psychomeiry," and clairvoyance.

### Tasking, targets, and protocols

The categories of tasking are penetration of inaccessible targets, science and technology information, cuing of intelligence collection systems, imminent hostilities, nuclear and non-nuclear targets, human source assessment, and personality profiles. The target categories are geographical areas or fixed sites, personalities, and search. Two standard forms are shown. The project form for Project STAR GATE carries fields for project number, viewer number, date, tasking, evaluation, and completion date. The report form carries fields for project number, viewer number, date, session date, and information. The session protocols are given as two options, solo and remote viewer with monitor. The closing slides state the programme's own doctrine for the use of its product: remote viewers can be used as collectors in conjunction with other intelligence sources throughout the Department of Defense intelligence community, and remote viewing information should not stand alone.
