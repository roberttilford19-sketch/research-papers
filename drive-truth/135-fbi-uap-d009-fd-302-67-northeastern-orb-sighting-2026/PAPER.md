## Abstract

This Federal Bureau of Investigation FD-302 records a February 2026 videoconference interview with a witness who reported observing red orbs at the witness's residence in the northeastern United States. According to the form's account, the witness returned home at approximately 9:15 p.m., noticed an intense bright light hovering below the tree line in the middle of the backyard, got out of the vehicle, and watched before retrieving a telephone to record video. A second person inside the home, who had seen the witness on the security camera standing by the vehicle looking into the backyard, came outside to join the observation. That person described the light as a red sphere about one meter in diameter, of a brilliant red never seen before, with what appeared at its center to be a white plasma sun the size of a basketball. The account states that the orb moved up and to the left, that a second identical orb was seen hovering above the first, and that the two moved together toward the west above the tree line, changing altitude and direction, silent and smooth, moving in tandem as if in formation or tethered, and appearing to merge into one as they moved out of sight. A video captured from the point when the orbs were above the trees is recorded as an attachment. The form also records a later observation, a few weeks afterward, of several white orbs travelling over the house from west to east at much higher altitude.

## Content Account

The form records an interview conducted in February 2026 by videoconference, with another person present. Biographical details of the principal witness are given in redacted form, including a bachelor's degree in economics and finance and a sequence of employments that are redacted.

The sighting account, as recorded, begins with the witness returning home at approximately 9:15 p.m. Eastern Time and, while pulling into a driveway that slopes downhill toward the residence, noticing an intense bright light hovering below the tree line in the middle of the backyard. The witness got out of the vehicle and stared at the light for a few moments before reaching back into the vehicle to retrieve a telephone to take a video. Meanwhile the second person, inside expecting the witness home, checked the security camera, saw the witness standing next to the vehicle looking into the backyard, and went outside to join. That person described the light as a red sphere about one meter in diameter, its red color brilliant and beautiful and unlike anything seen before, with, inside the sphere at its center, what was described as a white plasma sun about the size of a basketball.

As the two watched, the account states, the orb moved up and to their left, and they noticed a second, identical orb hovering above the first. The orbs then moved together toward the west above the tree line, exhibiting both changes in altitude and changes in direction, silent, moving smoothly and in tandem as if flying in formation or tethered together. As they moved out of sight, the witness saw the orbs merge together into one orb, or come so close together that only one could be seen. The two captured a video of the orbs beginning when they were above the trees and moving away, which is contained as an attachment to the interview document. The form closes by recording that a few weeks later the witness saw several white orbs travel over the house from west to east at much higher altitude than the red orbs, appearing the size of a dime held at arm's length.
