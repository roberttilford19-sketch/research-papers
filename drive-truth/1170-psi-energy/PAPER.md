## Abstract

This paper, by Duncan Blewett of the University of Regina and June C. Blewett of the Regina Mental Health Clinic, presents a theoretical model of psychic energy dynamics, offered as a progress report after the first decade of a project the authors say began in 1968 to examine the nature and potential applications of psychic energy. The model is stated as a series of related postulates. Each individual is a psychic energy system comprising mind and a psychic energy field. Each such system is in continuous inductive interaction with all others. The inductive process occurs in the unconscious mind and is the input, or source, of psychic energy for the individual's system. The induced energy then flows through the system and enters the conscious mind as the stream of consciousness, so that consciousness is the individual's experience of that flow. Psychic energy leaves the system through the expressive activities of being. The greater the throughput of energy, the greater the inductive effect of the individual's field, and the direction of flow within a system is determined by the individual's psychological set. The authors present these postulates as a theoretical structure within which, in their view, the workings of psychic energy can be understood and intentionally modified or directed.
