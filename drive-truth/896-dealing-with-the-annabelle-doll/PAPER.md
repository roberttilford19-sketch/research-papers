## Premise

This document records a single dowsing session the author conducted on June 22, 2025, concerning the Annabelle doll at the Warrens' Occult Museum in Monroe, Connecticut, after he heard a podcast about the doll being taken on a tour. It sets out the questions he asked his pendulum, the answers he recorded, the intervention he attempted on the strength of those answers, and the outcome he recorded afterward.

## Abstract

The document is a dated first-person account of a pendulum session. After hearing a podcast about the Annabelle doll, kept at the Warrens' Occult Museum in Monroe, Connecticut, and described as posing a threat to people during a tour across numerous states, the author dowsed the doll to determine whether it was possessed. He records that the pendulum answered yes, that a dowsing board indicated three to four spirits, and that the pendulum answered yes when he asked whether those spirits were demonic. He then describes closing his eyes and asking for help from his guardian angels, his spirit guides, and the archangels Raphael, Michael, and Gabriel. He offered the spirits a choice between going willingly with their assigned spirit guides into a higher spiritual dimension and being forced into an uncomfortable black box with nails sticking in it for an indefinite period, and he gave them three seconds to decide. Afterward he dowsed again and recorded that the attempt to transition the spirits had not succeeded, that the attempt to entrap them in the box had succeeded, and that the doll was no longer possessed. A closing note states that this was perhaps the first time his attempt to transition spirits had failed.

## Finished Text

Today is June 22 2025

After hearing a podcast regarding the infamous Annabelle doll located at the Warrens Occult Museum in Monroe Connecticut posing a threat to people during a “devils on the run tour” across numerous states, I decided to dowse the doll to determine if in fact it was possessed.

The pendulum answer was yes.

I dowsed how many spirits were possessing the doll

The board indicated 3-4 

Were these spirits demonic?

The answer was yes.

Whereupon I closed my eyes and asked for help from my guardian angels, spirit guides and Arch angels Raphael, Michael and Gabriel to directly intervene in this situation.

I asked that these spirits were given a choice between two options.

The first is that they voluntarily and willingly go with their assigned spirit guides into a higher spiritual dimension where they can grow spiritually …or, second, they will be forced into an uncomfortable black box with nails sticking in it for an indefinitely long period of time to prevent their haunting activities…

They have 3 seconds to decide

1,2,3 Go! 

After which I dowsed the following questions

Was I successful in transitioning these spirits to a higher spiritual dimension?

The pendulum indicated “no”...I was not 

Was I successful in entrapping them in an uncomfortable black block to prevent their continued haunting activities directed at people?

The answer was “yes”.

Is the Annabelle doll still possessed by spirits

The answer was no.

Are my guardian angels, Arch angels Raphael Michael and Gabriel as well as my spirit guides and volunteers aware of my thanks and appreciation for their help in dealing with this situation?

The answer was yes 

Note: This is perhaps the first time I was unsuccessful in transitioning spirits to a higher spiritual dimension…but instead entrapping them in an uncomfortable black box where they will remain so they can no longer harm people or feed off the fear of the living who visit that doll..
