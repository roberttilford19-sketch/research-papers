## Abstract

This Federal Bureau of Investigation electronic communication records an October 2024 interview with a witness who reported hundreds of observations of anomalous lights from the witness's property in the northeastern United States. According to the communication, the witness's first sighting in the case occurred in 2021, at about 5:00 in the morning, when lights seen through a back window were first taken for someone in the yard with a flashlight; outside, the witness observed a single light that split into two or three lights forming a triangular pattern with a light in the center, remaining for approximately ten minutes, and disappearing when a flash photograph was accidentally taken while filming. The communication records discussion of a March 2022 sighting shown on video, the witness's statement that the lights typically appear at dusk or at night and appear to morph and move laterally, and the witness's account of reaching out to a former official through a professional networking site and being told the observations were legitimate and that people have been hurt by these things. It also records a 1996 incident the witness described, in which a vehicle travelling from Fort Bliss, Texas, to Fort Carson, Colorado, shut down in New Mexico under a large light for about two minutes, and the witness's method of checking sightings against automatic dependent surveillance broadcast data to exclude aircraft.

## Content Account

The interview, conducted in October 2024, begins from a presentation and videos previously provided to the Bureau. The witness, employed at a facility identified only in redacted form, reported having spoken with fellow and former employees about the sightings observed from the property.

The sighting history recorded is detailed. The witness's first sighting in the case was in 2021: exercising at home at about 5:00 a.m., the witness noticed lights outside the back window, first believed someone was in the yard with a flashlight, went outside, and observed a singular light that eventually split into two or three lights forming a triangular pattern with a light in the center, remaining for approximately ten minutes. Another person was brought to see it, and while filming on a smartphone the witness accidentally took a flash photograph, at which the lights immediately disappeared. Speaking to a sighting in March 2022, the witness played a previously provided video and explained that its location differed from the usual location, and stated that the observed lights typically appear at dusk or at night and appear to morph and move laterally.

The communication further records that the witness first reached out through LinkedIn to a named-in-redaction former official to discuss the observations, and was told that what the witness was observing was legitimate and that people have been hurt by these things, with advice to be careful. Asked about any earlier experience, the witness described a 1996 incident: driving with an Army officer from Fort Bliss, Texas, to Fort Carson, Colorado, the vehicle shut down in New Mexico while a large light shone on them; after about two minutes the light went away and the vehicle's electronics returned. The witness reported having seen hundreds of these lights from the property, with numerous videos and photographs available to share, increased activity since September, shorter bursts of activity, and frequent corrupted video when recording. The closing portion records the witness's deconfliction method: cross-referencing timestamps against automatic dependent surveillance broadcast records and checking the azimuth of the lights to determine which sightings might be aircraft, together with the witness's acknowledgment of never having had an interest in the subject and that much of the account may seem strange.
