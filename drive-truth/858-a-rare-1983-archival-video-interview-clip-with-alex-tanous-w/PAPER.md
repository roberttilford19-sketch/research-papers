## Content account

The document's statements about the experiment are the summary's account of what Tanous said in the interview; they are his claims, presented here as his claims.

According to the document, Tanous described the experimental arrangement as follows. His physical body stayed in an isolation chamber while his projected double traveled about fifty feet, through what he described as five doors, to a room containing a strain gauge box. The strain gauge was to measure his physical presence at that location, and the readings were to be registered alongside an EEG recording so that any perturbation of the gauges could be correlated with his brainwave activity at the same moment. In parallel, the double was to read targets chosen randomly by push button. The targets were optical illusions; the summary notes a wheel with four colors and a series of five pictures shown at random. Tanous emphasized, in the summary's account, that he had not seen the equipment or the targets in advance and knew only the general arrangement, because foreknowledge of what would be randomly selected would undermine the test.

The document quotes Tanous directly at length once: "In this experiment, I will be projecting my consciousness (Alex 2), 50 ft away into a strain gauge box, which will measure my physical presence. At the same time, I will be reading targets that they will select randomly by push button which I will be reading to show that I'm actually there. In these two facets, it will show you how I leave my body and what can be done with it and therefore it will be registered on the EEG as we go along."

The summary also records Tanous's description of how his ability had changed over time. Alex 2, he said, was no longer merely a light projection, as it had been in the beginning, but had become "a physical double just like me." From the isolation chamber, he said, he could observe Alex 2 performing the tasks and could see the other room, which was "all open" to him. He further attributed irregularities during filming — a red camera light flashing, the picture jumping, and a pager going off — to his energy being "very very high" during the experiment, and he recalled an earlier television appearance in which his image failed to develop on film while his voice was recorded, which he likewise linked to his energy.

The document closes with two framing statements: that the clip ends abruptly and the final results of the experiment are unclear, and that the experiment was one of many in which Tanous participated for the American Society for Psychical Research during the 1980s.
