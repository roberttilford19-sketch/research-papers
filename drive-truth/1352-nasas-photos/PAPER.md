## Premise

The document was produced to circulate, within the Central Intelligence Agency, a notice of a technical meeting on Apollo lunar photographic work, and to expedite that circulation. The attached notice was produced to inform members and guests of the meeting's programme: a showing of the official National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) film of the Apollo 12 flight and talks by the three members of a photographic team advising NASA on scientific imaging requirements for future Apollo missions.

## Abstract

This two-page item consists of a Central Intelligence Agency official routing slip dated February 1970, bearing the handwritten instruction to expedite, followed by a technical meeting notice issued by the Potomac Region of the American Society of Photogrammetry. The meeting was scheduled for Monday evening, 9 February 1970, at the National Academy of Sciences in Washington, under the subject heading of what Apollo had accomplished, where it was going, and where it should be going. The programme comprised a colour showing of the official NASA film of the Apollo 12 expedition and presentations by three speakers identified as members of the Apollo Lunar Exploration Orbital Science Photographic Team. Leon Kosofsky of NASA was to describe the photographic subsystems then in use, the type and quality of data being acquired, and to present stereo slides of the lunar surface. Donald Light of TOPOCOM was to review the reduction of Lunar Orbiter photography and the products being generated from Orbiter and Apollo photography. Frederick Doyle of the United States Geological Survey was to describe plans being pursued for future Apollo missions. The notice lists the region's officers and the co-sponsoring societies, and invites interested guests. The Drive filename refers to the photographic subject matter; the document itself is a meeting notice, not a set of photographs.

## Content Account

The routing slip is a standard Central Intelligence Agency form, with classification check boxes and action categories, carrying the release marking of a sanitised copy approved for release on 25 March 2010 under the identifier CIA-RDP80T01137A000200030039-4, as rendered in the OCR text. Its remarks consist essentially of the request to expedite, with a February 1970 date. The meeting notice names the Potomac Region officers, gives the date, time, and place of the meeting, and describes each speaker's subject and background in turn. Kosofsky's background is given as including four years at NASA as programme engineer for the Lunar Orbiter programme, five years in mapping development at the Army Map Service, and eight years at an aerial reconnaissance laboratory at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base. Light's background is given as more than fifteen years in analytical photogrammetry and mapping, rising to chief of an extraterrestrial branch responsible for lunar and Martian photogrammetric reduction and mapping. Doyle is described as national president of the society and chairman of NASA's photographic team. The co-sponsors named are the Washington chapters of the Society of Motion Picture and Television Engineers and the Society of Photographic Scientists and Engineers.
