## Abstract

This Federal Bureau of Investigation electronic communication records an evening visit by two special agents to the witness's home in the northeastern United States orb case in November 2024, for the purpose of confirming the reports and video the witness had previously provided. According to the communication, conditions were approximately 48 degrees, overcast, with no precipitation. From the patio on the north side of the home, the witness first pointed out lights believed to be homes and permanent structures. Approximately twenty minutes later, one agent observed a white pulsation of light that appeared, moved horizontally with somewhat erratic movements for less than ten seconds without reflecting off the water or illuminating the area below it, and disappeared. The communication further records repeated white lights at the top of the western tree line that pulsed and flashed a white-blue hue, plumes of broad light appearing for several seconds before flashes, and, at approximately 5:30 p.m., an observation by both agents of a bright white light above a bright red light that hovered at about treetop height, moved quickly from right to left, disappeared, reappeared at its starting point, and repeated the movement. It records that five photographs were taken but were mostly blurry, and that observation continued until about 6:10 p.m.

## Content Account

The details section states the visit's purpose: to confirm reports and video the witness had previously provided of unidentified aerial phenomena over the last three years. Two special agents visited the home on a November 2024 date redacted in part, in weather of approximately 48 degrees, overcast, with no precipitation. At approximately 4:45 p.m., agents and the witness went to the patio on the north side of the home, where the witness pointed out lights in the distance believed to be homes and other permanent structures, and lights about eight miles away whose sources he could not identify with certainty.

Approximately twenty minutes later, one agent observed what he believed to be an unidentified phenomenon in the direction of a redacted landmark: a white pulsation of light appeared at the northern edge of the feature and began to move horizontally with somewhat erratic movements. The communication records that the light did not appear to reflect off the water, giving the possibility that its source was over the wooded area to the north; it did not illuminate the area below it or the water's surface; it appeared at an altitude at or below the tree line; and it danced around for less than ten seconds, moving side to side, before disappearing.

Within the western tree line, the communication records further lights observed by the agents on multiple occasions: white lights appearing at the top of the tree line, pulsing and flashing a white-blue hue before disappearing, generally stationary; and plumes of broad light appearing for several seconds before a flash of light, red or white, that moved erratically before disappearing. One agent attempted photographs; five were taken, but they were mostly blurry and provided little context, and it was later determined that a tripod or other stable platform would be required. At approximately 5:15 p.m. the witness's family members arrived home. At approximately 5:30 p.m., both agents observed a bright white light above a bright red light at the far end of the pond, hovering at approximately treetop height, which moved quickly from right to left, disappeared, then reappeared simultaneously where it had first appeared, moved quickly from right to left again, and disappeared. Observation continued until approximately 6:10 p.m., and the agents departed at approximately 6:15 p.m.
