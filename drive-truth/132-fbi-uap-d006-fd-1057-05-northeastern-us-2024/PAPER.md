## Abstract

This Federal Bureau of Investigation electronic communication records a site survey, dated 1 November 2024, of the property of the witness in the northeastern United States orb case. According to the communication, the witness provided a tour of the property to give perspective to recordings previously provided, and took agents into a wooded area approximately 200 yards from the home, where a trail camera that had captured reported activity was affixed facing west. The communication notes signs restricting hunting and fishing in the wooded area and a stone wall running south to north. It records that the witness showed agents around the home and indicated the areas where activity had been observed, that a trail runs around a nearby feature but not through the area of the reported activity, and that multiple homes along the road to the east of the witness's home would have line of sight to the area where the witness had observed activity. It further records that the witness showed agents a video, provided a copy of material to them, and stated openness to equipment being set up on the property for time-lapse video and to coordinating an evening visit when activity is often observed. Twenty-four enclosures are listed, principally tree line photographs taken west of the home, trail camera photographs, and photographs from the back deck, together with a photo log.
