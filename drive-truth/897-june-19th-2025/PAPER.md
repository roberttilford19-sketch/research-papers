## Abstract

In this dated first-person entry, Robert Tilford describes dowsing a house in Osborne, Kansas, remotely, before ever visiting it. A friend who had lived there reported hearing footsteps upstairs at night, and a later check found no one upstairs, which led Tilford to suspect a spirit presence. Working with a pendulum and a pendulum board, he concluded that two spirits were present and that they were neither malevolent nor demonic. Rather than forcing them out, he describes initiating a remote intervention in which his guardian angels, spirit guides, and spirit volunteers were asked to invite the two spirits, gently and without compulsion, to cross over voluntarily to a higher spiritual dimension, with loved ones they could see and trust explaining that they had died and that no harm would come to them. He records that the pendulum answered yes when he asked whether the offer had been accepted, yes when he asked whether the spirits had transitioned safely and voluntarily, and no when he asked whether any spirits remained in the home. He then used the pendulum to change the frequency of the house to one of positivity for any future buyers. The entry closes with his judgment that the work was a remote intervention rather than an exorcism, and with his report that he felt no disturbing energies when he visited the house later that day.
