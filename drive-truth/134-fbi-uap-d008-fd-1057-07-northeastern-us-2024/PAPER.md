## Abstract

This Federal Bureau of Investigation electronic communication, dated 1 December 2024, records a daylight survey of the area around the location in the northeastern United States orb case where special agents had observed unidentified aerial phenomenon activity in November 2024. According to the communication, the agents walked the perimeter on a marked trail of approximately two miles in a clockwise direction, beginning at about 10:30 a.m., and left the trail in areas to the west and north. On the northwest side, at approximately 11:00 a.m., they descended to the water's edge and confirmed an unobstructed line of sight to the witness's property. In the vicinity where the agents had viewed activity the previous month, they found nothing unusual present on the ground or at treetop level; on the north side they noted an area that appeared to have once been used as a fire pit, with no evidence of recent burns, the ground being blanketed with leaves. The communication records that tree cover throughout the area was moderate, that the agents had line of sight to multiple homes to the east, and that the agents assessed it would be unrealistic for someone to fly a drone or other craft at night in the area without risking collision with tree limbs. Photographs taken around the location on an agent's Bureau-issued telephone are listed as enclosures.
