## Abstract

This document is Robert Tilford's own record of a strange telephone call with CM on Wednesday night, May 29, 2025. During the hour-long call he heard a descending "whaa whaa" sad-trombone sound, and CM confirmed that she had heard it too, although each of them had assumed it came from the other's end and Robert is clear that the sound was localised inside his phone rather than in his room. Later in the same call he heard a fart sound that CM did not hear, and for about a minute he could hear nothing from her side at all while the call timer showed the call still connected; afterwards CM told him she had been speaking and could hear him calling out. Robert records his reasoning that something was interfering selectively with the audio at each end. His notes identify the sad trombone as the "Womp Womp" effect and report his search finding that the fart sound matches the Audio Emoji feature in the Google Phone app on Android, while noting that he was using an iPhone SE 3 on TracFone and CM an Android phone. An update records his pendulum dowsing, his conclusion that a troublesome earthbound spirit had attached to the phone, and a remote exorcism. A follow-up dated June 8, 2025 records applause and the same trombone sound on a call with Cindy, on a different phone and a different carrier, and a further note dated June 10, 2025 records his dowsing and exorcism concerning Cindy.
