# Sources — Uma' Lasan (xky)

Pointers only. This file cites the published sources and catalogues behind the Uma' Lasan held-language pack; it reproduces none of their text. Anything quoted in the pack's documents is quoted there, briefly, with its source named in the same breath.

## The fragments of record

- Ray, Sidney H. "The Languages of Borneo." *Sarawak Museum Journal* 1(4), 1913. Comparative lists of 211 words explicitly including Uma' Lasan (as "Kinjin/Kindjin"); digital via BHL/Zenodo: https://explore.openaire.eu/search/result?pid=10.5281/zenodo.16041147 — the oldest and largest single fragment in hand.
- Soriente, Antonia. *A classification of Kenyah languages in Sarawak and East Kalimantan.* UKM dissertation, 2003 (400 pp.). The only modern source treating Uma' Lasan with wordlist data. Library-only; no lawful digital copy found.

## Audio without a lexicon

- Global Recordings Network — Uma' Lasan language page: https://globalrecordings.net/en/language/xky — audio Bible-teaching only. No lexical resource, and no scripture text in any form: portions, New Testament, and Bible all "None Reported" in every catalogue checked.

## The catalogues of record (all searched; no dedicated resource found)

- Ethnologue — entry for Uma' Lasan [xky]: https://www.ethnologue.com/language/xky — lists the language "Unwritten"; records ~80% lexical similarity to the Badeng/Lepu Kulit dialects of Mainstream Kenyah [xkl].
- Glottolog — entry for Uma' Lasan at https://glottolog.org; its bibliography holds no Uma' Lasan dictionary or grammar.
- OLAC (Open Language Archives Community) — Uma' Lasan records at http://www.language-archives.org/language/xky
- SIL Archives, PARADISEC, Kaipuleohone, archive.org, SEAlang, WorldCat, Glosbe, ASJP, Joshua Project — each searched in the hunts recorded in this pack; none holds a dedicated Uma' Lasan lexical resource.
