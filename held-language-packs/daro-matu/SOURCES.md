# Sources — Daro-Matu (dro)

Pointers only. This file cites the published sources and catalogues behind the Daro-Matu held-language pack; it reproduces none of their text. Anything quoted in the pack's documents is quoted there, briefly, with its source named in the same breath.

## The published record of the language

- Rensch, Calvin R. *Melanau and the Languages of Central Sarawak.* SIL Electronic Survey Reports 2012-011. SIL International, 2012: https://www.sil.org/resources/archives/47763 — treats Matu-Daro explicitly; cognate sets with Matu forms throughout. The partial base in hand.
- Chou, Grace. *A Reconstruction of Proto-Melanau.* Thesis, 2002. Eleven varieties including Matu; the source of much of Rensch's Matu material. No lawful digital copy found — library holdings only.
- Ray, Sidney H. "The Languages of Borneo." *Sarawak Museum Journal* 1(4), 1913, pp. 1–196. Short historical wordlist material covering the Melanau varieties.
- ASJP (Automated Similarity Judgment Program) — short Daro-Matu wordlist, in hand digitally; very short.

## Neighbouring records that are NOT this language's base

- *Kamus Bahasa Melanau Mukah – Melayu Dewan.* Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka, Cawangan Sarawak. Covers Mukah — ISO mel, not dro. Library/purchase print.
- *Melanau Ajang–Malay Dictionary* (online, ~4,300 words, built on Zaini Oje's DBP Sarawak manuscript): https://www.melanau.org/about.html — covers Ajang/Rajang, Matu's closest sister. Recorded so it is never mistaken for a dro base.
- Clayre, I. F. — 1972 Edinburgh thesis on Melanau. Library-only; not Matu-Daro-specific.
- A *Kamus Bahasa Melanau Matu Daro* circulates only as an upload of unverified provenance on a document-sharing site. Excluded from the base; recorded here so the title is not later mistaken for a checked source.
