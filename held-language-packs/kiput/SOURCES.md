# Sources — Kiput (kyi)

Pointers only. This file cites the published sources and catalogues behind the Kiput held-language pack; it reproduces none of their text. Anything quoted in the pack's documents is quoted there, briefly, with its source named in the same breath.

## The published record of the language

- Blust, Robert. *A short morphology, phonology and vocabulary of Kiput, Sarawak.* Pacific Linguistics 546. Canberra: The Australian National University, 2003. viii + 102 pp. ISBN 0 85883 536 3. DOI: https://doi.org/10.15144/PL-546 — the book is openly available as a PDF through the ANU Open Research Repository: https://openresearch-repository.anu.edu.au/items/005e2411-1145-48fc-b36a-3c3791fd75f0 (online edition licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, 2015). WorldCat: OCLC 54984089. This is the sole published Kiput lexicon and the pack's base of record.
- Blust, Robert. Field notebook on the languages of northern Sarawak, 1971 — the Kiput section at pages 61–81. Digitised page scans, freely downloadable: Kaipuleohone, University of Hawai'i ScholarSpace, item RB2-003-B, handle 10125/33160. The raw elicitation from which PL-546 was distilled; the same data, not a second source. An audio recording from the same 1971 fieldwork is catalogued at PARADISEC as RB1-003.
- Blust, Robert. "Kiput Historical Phonology." *Oceanic Linguistics* 41(2), 2002. A phonology paper on the same 1971 data; carries no lexicon beyond PL-546. Library holdings (subscription journal).
- Ray, Sidney H. "The Languages of Borneo." *Sarawak Museum Journal* 1, 1913. Contains the first Long Kiput wordlist, a little over 200 items in an early transcription — a smaller, superseded subset of PL-546. Research-library holdings.

## The catalogues of record

- Ethnologue — entry for Kiput [kyi]: https://www.ethnologue.com/language/kyi. Carries the speaker count (~450), the EGIDS 6b (Threatened) grade, the language-development listing ("Dictionary. Grammar." — both items are PL-546), and the writing status "Unwritten."
- Glottolog 5.4 — entry for Kiput (kyi) at https://glottolog.org. Its bibliography for the language is five items long: the four published items above and the Ethnologue catalogue entry. Nothing else exists in the scholarly bibliography of record.
- Joshua Project — people group 12710 (Kiput of Malaysia): https://joshuaproject.net/people_groups/12710, and its language record for kyi. Bible Portions, New Testament, and Complete Bible all "None Reported"; "No reported resources in this language." Its "Translation Started" status flag names no portion, agency, year, or text, and is recorded in the pack so that it is never mistaken for a portion.
- Global Recordings Network — Kiput language listing; holds no Kiput recording.
- Scripture Earth — no Kiput page exists (checked 2026-11-17).
- OLAC (Open Language Archives Community) — lists for Kiput only PL-546, the Glottolog and Ethnologue entries, and Blust's 1971 deposits. SEAlang holds no Kiput dictionary. WorldCat, Open Library, and CiNii each hold the single Kiput title, PL-546.

## Community and mission context

- Diocese of Miri — https://rcdmiri.org (history and communities pages). Records the Catholic mission at Kuala Tutoh, whose 106th anniversary was celebrated in December 2017, and profiles the Kiput ("Lakiput," self-name *Lepo Pu'un*) at about 2,000 people. No Kiput-language publication of the diocese was found in any catalogue.
- "Sociocultural Perspective Among the Kiput of Baram." *Tamaddun* journal, March 2026. Peer-reviewed ethnography of the Kuala Tutoh and Kampung Benawa settlements; records Sunday worship services conducted in the Kiput language at Kuala Tutoh, and the community's 2024 resolution on the Kiput name and its 2025 *Adiit Mucu'ut Kiput* heritage festival.
- Bible Society of Malaysia and United Bible Societies channels — searched for any Kiput portion, lectionary, or hymnal; none exists in their catalogues (2026-11-17).
