# Semelai (sza) — extracted term list, gate re-run of 2026-11-17

Every form below was read at the desk on 2026-11-17 from the Aslian etymological database (Starling, starlingdb.org, database "asliet"), which compiles published Aslian sources; all 304 records of the database were read through (16 pages). The database's Semelai column is the only lawfully open Semelai lexical base in hand: Kruspe 2004's vocabulary (pp. 448–465 of *A Grammar of Semelai*, Cambridge University Press) is purchase-only and is not in hand, and the Semelai Mark / New Testament text (Faith Comes By Hearing app; Bible Brain) is not reachable in bulk through any lawful open channel the desk could walk tonight (Bible Brain API answered 403, key required; Scripture Earth's Semelai page was rate-limited and not retried; YouVersion has no Semelai language page).

79 distinct attested meanings. No religious term of the desk's core list (God, Lord, heaven, hell, angel, spirit, pray, mercy, prophet, soul, bless, holy, sin, forgive) is attested anywhere in this base — each is recorded NOT ATTESTED in the report, not filled in.
