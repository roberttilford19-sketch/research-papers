# Sources — Semelai (sza)

Pointers only. This file cites the published sources and catalogues behind the Semelai held-language pack; it reproduces none of their text. Anything quoted in the pack's documents is quoted there, briefly, with its source named in the same breath.

## The published Scripture

- Wycliffe Malaysia, "God Speaks Semelai!" — https://wycliffe.my (2022, updated 2024). The translation team's own account: "We are celebrating the completion of Mark Gospel in Semelai!"; the celebration at Temerloh Gospel Chapel, 15 January 2022, with the Bible Society of Malaysia and Faith Comes By Hearing present; audio Mark and a LUMO Mark film launched 19 February 2022; three Semelai translators then working on Luke. The article quotes no Semelai verse.
- Faith Comes By Hearing, "Semelai Bible" app — presents a New Testament with synchronized text and audio; describes itself as "New Testament with Audio." The app's text is not readable by a text fetch.
- Scripture Earth — Semelai page: https://scriptureearth.org — answered rate-limited (HTTP 429) on 2026-11-17 and was not retried, as that answer instructs.
- Bible Brain (Faith Comes By Hearing) — the Bible.is text store; its public API is key-gated (answered 403, key required, 2026-11-17).
- YouVersion / Bible.com — no Semelai language page exists (404, checked 2026-11-17).
- Joshua Project — Semelai listings: the older page (Bible Portions 2021; New Testament "None Reported") is stale against the app; a prayer card now lists "Scripture: Complete Bible," recorded in this pack as a discrepancy against the verified extent (Mark + the app's presented New Testament), not as settling it.
- PeopleGroups.org, PG009335 (Semelai).

## The scholarly base

- Kruspe, Nicole. *A Grammar of Semelai.* Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2004. The Semelai–English vocabulary is its appendix, pp. 448–465 — the largest Semelai wordlist in print. Purchase-only; not in hand. (No standalone Kruspe Semelai dictionary exists; her published dictionary is of Mah Meri, 2010.) The one openly quotable window into the book is its pronoun table (p. 171) as reproduced in a 2017 Hamburg handout: https://staff.germanistik.rub.de
- Aslian etymological database (Starling) — https://starlingdb.org, database "asliet"; the Semelai column (79 attested meanings), all 304 records read 2026-11-17. The pack's lexical base in hand.
- SEAlang Mon-Khmer Database — carries a searchable extraction of Kruspe's Semelai vocabulary ("Semelai, all 1336 items (Kru2004)"): http://sealang.net/monkhmer/database/byBranchAndLang.htm — catalogued in the base hunt; the gate re-run's count was run on the etymological database column in hand, and this extraction is named here as a further pointer, not as a counted source.
- Burenhult, Niclas. *A Grammar of Jahai.* Pacific Linguistics 566, The Australian National University, 2005 — the source of the assessment of Kruspe's grammar quoted in the pack ("the most comprehensive description of any Aslian language").
