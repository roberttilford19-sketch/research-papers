## The language

Semelai (ISO 639-3: sza) is a Southern Aslian language — Austroasiatic, not Austronesian — spoken by an Orang Asli people of Peninsular Malaysia, in Pahang. Its published description is the strongest of any held lane's: Nicole Kruspe's *A Grammar of Semelai* (Cambridge University Press, 2004), which Niclas Burenhult's *Grammar of Jahai* (Pacific Linguistics, 2005) calls "the most comprehensive description of any Aslian language."
