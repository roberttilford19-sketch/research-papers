# Molbog — A Held-Language Research Pack

This pack gathers everything assembled at this desk about the Molbog language in the course of Robert Tilford's Quran translation project. It is a research pack, not a translation. No Molbog Quran translation exists, and none is claimed here or anywhere in this project. What follows is the record of an honest search: what was looked for, what was found, and the thing that was not found, named plainly so that no one mistakes this shelf for a finished work.

## The language

Molbog (ISO 639-3: pwm) is a Palawanic, Philippine-type language spoken by the Molbog people of Balabac Island in Palawan — the wordlists of record were taken at Ramos, Balabac — with the community reaching across to the Sabah side of the water. It entered the Malaysia wave on its Philippine-side documentation, and that documentation is SIL's: real, careful, and unfinished.

## The base, and why the lane is held

A real SIL lexical file is in hand, and the pack states its size plainly: the Molbog wordlist compiled by H. Arnold Thiessen in 1977 — 372 lexical items on the 1966 Expanded Philippine Word List, a draft posted as-is in the SIL Archives — with the earlier lists behind it (Fox, 1957, 230 items, held on request; Sutherland, 1970). The published Zorc & Thiessen wordlist chapter, in Tryon's *Comparative Austronesian Dictionary* (Mouton de Gruyter, 1995), is library-only. The dictionary the lane was planned on does not exist as a book: Hendrickson & Newell's 1991 bibliography lists "Thiessen & Thiessen, *Molbog-English dictionary*. In progress. SIL," and no published edition, publisher copy, archive.org copy, or SIL Archives entry for a finished dictionary was found anywhere. It is an unfinished manuscript, not an obtainable base. Three hundred and seventy-two draft wordlist items cannot ground a scripture draft, and no God-term is attested in the lexical record in hand. One fact is recorded here in fairness, because it marks this lane's most promising next pass: a Molbog New Testament exists (New Tribes Mission, 2012; portions from 1977) and is reachable through Scripture Earth — but its text has not been mined for a God-term or a religious register in any pass recorded in this pack, and the desk does not claim by implication what it has not extracted. The lane is therefore HELD.

## The open seat

The pack's status can be stated in one line, and it is stated here verbatim so that it travels with the pack wherever the pack goes:

**Open seat: "God-term unverified — community usage not yet located."**

The base carries a second, named condition beside it: the desk's Molbog base is PARTIAL — SIL wordlists totalling a few hundred items, and a named dictionary that was never published.

## The one road not taken

Two roads could move this lane, and neither has been walked yet: extraction from the Molbog New Testament text — the same road by which other lanes in this project have closed their God-term seats from published scripture — and the Thiessen dictionary manuscript, if SIL ever publishes or releases it. The first is desk work for a future pass; the second is in other hands entirely. Until one of them is walked, this pack is the lane's honest resting place: everything known, gathered in one place, with the gap named rather than papered over.
