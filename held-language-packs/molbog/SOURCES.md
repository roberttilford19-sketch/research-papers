# Sources — Molbog (pwm)

Pointers only. This file cites the published sources and catalogues behind the Molbog held-language pack; it reproduces none of their text. Anything quoted in the pack's documents is quoted there, briefly, with its source named in the same breath.

## The published record of the language

- Thiessen, H. Arnold (comp.). *Molbog wordlist (1977).* SIL Philippines / SIL Archives entry 80581: https://philippines.sil.org/resources/archives/80581 — 372 lexical items (1966 Expanded Philippine Word List; Molbog of Ramos, Balabac), PDF linked from the entry; a draft, posted as-is. The base in hand. (The Grok return graded in this pack cites the related SIL Archives entry 80576 for the expanded list and supplements.)
- Molbog wordlist (1957), Fox — 230 items, SIL Archives entry 80574. Library-only / on request ("Not Available Online"). A further list (Sutherland, 1970) stands in the same SIL series.
- Zorc, R. David & Thiessen, H. Arnold. "Molbog: introduction and wordlist." In Tryon (ed.), *Comparative Austronesian Dictionary.* Mouton de Gruyter, 1995, pp. 359–362. Library-only.
- Thiessen & Thiessen. *Molbog-English dictionary.* Listed as "In progress. SIL" in Hendrickson & Newell (1991). No published edition exists in any catalogue searched — an unfinished manuscript, not an obtainable base.

## Scripture

- Molbog New Testament (New Tribes Mission, 2012; portions from 1977), reachable via Scripture Earth's pwm page, which links Bible.is text/audio and the JESUS Film: https://www.scriptureearth.org/00eng.php?sortby=lang&iso=pwm — recorded as the lane's most promising extraction road. Its text has not been mined for a God-term in any pass recorded in this pack. No separate ebible.org pwm listing was returned.
