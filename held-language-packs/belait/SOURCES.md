# Sources — Belait (beg)

Pointers only. This file cites the published sources and catalogues behind the Belait held-language pack; it reproduces none of their text. Anything quoted in the pack's documents is quoted there, briefly, with its source named in the same breath.

## The published record of the language

- Abdullah, Noor Alifah. *Struktur Bahasa Belait.* Universiti Brunei Darussalam BA thesis, 1992; published as a book by Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka Brunei, 2004 (204 pp. per the Glottolog catalogue entry). Print only; no lawful digital copy found in the UBD repository, archive.org, or ELAR (checked 2026-11-17). The lane's named base.
- *Kamus dialek Belait – Melayu.* Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka Brunei, first edition, 2025. ISBN 9789998400832 (Michigan eLibrary catalogue record). A Belait–Malay dictionary in print; not in hand at this desk. Located by catalogue record in the Grok research return graded in this pack.
- *Daftar Leksikal 7 Dialek Brunei Darussalam.* Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka, 2011. Carries Belait lexical material among the seven Brunei dialects; referenced in secondary literature, full digital access not confirmed.
- Clynes, Adrian. "Belait." In Adelaar & Himmelmann (eds.), *The Austronesian Languages of Asia and Madagascar.* Routledge, 2005. The standard sketch; purchase-or-library only.
- Drayton, Holly. "Lower Baram Varieties of Brunei and Sarawak." ELAR (SOAS) collection 0662: https://www.elararchive.org/dk0662/ — 30+ hours of Belait audio-visual data, ~16 hours transcribed, ~5 hours glossed; item-level access conditions apply. The partial digital base in hand.
- Clynes, Adrian (2012), *Language Documentation & Conservation* 6 (open access), and Drayton (2025), SEALS proceedings (open access) — genuine glossed Belait data; partial, no lexicon.
- Austronesian Basic Vocabulary Database (ABVD) — Belait wordlist, ~211 items (Peter Mastin): https://abvd.eva.mpg.de/austronesian/language.php?id=151

## The catalogues of record

- Ethnologue — entry for Belait [beg]: https://www.ethnologue.com/language/beg
- Glottolog — entry for Belait (beg) at https://glottolog.org; its bibliography carries the *Struktur Bahasa Belait* as a 204-page grammar sketch.
- OLAC (Open Language Archives Community) — Belait records at http://www.language-archives.org/language/beg
- Scripture Earth and the Bible-society channels — no Belait page, portion, or text exists in their catalogues (checked 2026-11-17). Joshua Project and Global Recordings Network list the language; no Belait scripture resource was located by any search recorded in this pack.
