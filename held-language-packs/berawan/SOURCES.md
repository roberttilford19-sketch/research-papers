# Sources — Berawan (zbc / zbw)

Pointers only. This file cites the published sources and catalogues behind the Berawan held-language pack; it reproduces none of their text. Anything quoted in the pack's documents is quoted there, briefly, with its source named in the same breath.

## The published record of the language

- Burkhardt, Jürgen M., Burkhardt, Jey L. & Ang Lay Hoon. *Comparative Berawan Glossary / Glosari Komparatif Bahasa Berawan.* Penerbit UM (University of Malaya Press), 2023. ~4,800 items, ~1,200 per lect across Batu Belah [zbc], Long Teru [zbc], Long Jegan [zbe], Long Terawan [zbw], in a unified community orthography. The dictionary base for zbc and zbw alike. Class: PURCHASE-ONLY (out of stock at the seller checked, 2026-11-17). Not purchased.
- Burkhardt, Jürgen M. — Proto-Berawan dissertation, 2014 (425–466 items per lect). Circulates as author self-archive only; flagged in the hunt, not counted as a repository copy.
- UNIMAS *Journal of Borneo-Kalimantan* (open access) — the unified-orthography paper (2020) and the 2025 glossary paper with a reproduced glossary excerpt: https://publisher.unimas.my/ojs/index.php/BJK/article/download/2411/1012
- ABVD — Berawan (Long Terawan, zbw) wordlist, 216 items: https://abvd.eva.mpg.de/austronesian/language.php?id=45&history=all&sort=item
- Austronesian Comparative Dictionary — Berawan (Long Terawan) entries, scattered: https://ling.lll.hawaii.edu/dicts/ACD/acd-lo_r.htm
- Blust, Robert — 1971 field notebooks, Kaipuleohone (open): Long Teru (zbc), http://hdl.handle.net/10125/33159 ; Batu Belah (zbc), http://hdl.handle.net/10125/33161

## The religious record

- Metcalf, Peter. *Where Are You, Spirits: Style and Theme in Berawan Prayer.* Smithsonian Institution Press, 1989. The standard study of Berawan prayer; source of *bili* "spirit" and *papi'*, the prayer genre.
- Metcalf, Peter. *A Borneo Journey into Death.* 1982. Funeral rites and eschatology; soul and ancestor terminology (*sadt*; *ukun*, *dupun/tepun*).
- Earlier wordlists: Ray (1913), Proctor (1977–78), Blust — minimal religious coverage.

## Scripture

- Central Berawan (zbc): none reported anywhere.
- West Berawan (zbw): portions dated 2005–2011 stand in the record, but no lawful digital text was found on ebible.org, Bible.is, YouVersion, or Scripture Earth (checked 2026-11-17). No Berawan hymnal or catechism was located by any search recorded in this pack.
