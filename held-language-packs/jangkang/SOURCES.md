# Sources — Jangkang (djo)

Pointers only. This file cites the published sources and catalogues behind the Jangkang held-language pack; it reproduces none of their text. Anything quoted in the pack's documents is quoted there, briefly, with its source named in the same breath.

## The fragments of record

- Universitas Tanjungpura journal wordlists — among them 41 Bokidoh Dayak Jangkang rice terms with forms and meanings, open access: https://jurnal.untan.ac.id/index.php/jpdpb/article/view/54540 ; a companion semantic-relations study (GARUDA-indexed) is abstract-level only.

## Scripture state

- WorldBibles — records that the Bible is not yet translated into Jangkang.
- Joshua Project — Jangkang language record: translation "Started," portions "None Reported": https://joshuaproject.net/languages/djo
- Global Recordings Network — audio teaching only ("Good News — Jangkang"). ebible.org and bible.is searches for djo returned no listing.

## The catalogues of record (all searched; no lexical resource found)

- OLAC (Open Language Archives Community) — Jangkang page: http://www.language-archives.org/language/djo — lists only Glottolog and Ethnologue as language descriptions; no lexical resource at all.
- Ethnologue — entry for Jangkang [djo]: https://www.ethnologue.com/language/djo
- Glottolog — entry for Jangkang at https://glottolog.org; no Jangkang dictionary or grammar in its bibliography.
- Badan Bahasa / Balai Bahasa Kalimantan Barat, Perpusnas-style catalogues, SIL Indonesia, archive.org, Google Books, SEAlang, WorldCat, Glosbe — each searched in the hunts recorded in this pack; no *Kamus Bahasa Jangkang*, *Struktur Bahasa Jangkang*, or equivalent volume exists in any of them.
