# Memorial and Dedication — The DNA Encodings of 14 November 2026

Written at Hays, Kansas, on the day the work was done, by Robert Tilford, and given free.

## Dedication

This day's work is dedicated to the memory of **Francis Harry Compton Crick** (1916–2004) — who, with James Watson, saw the shape of the molecule that carries every living thing, and who wrote, with a restraint this house admires, the most consequential understatement in science: *"It has not escaped our notice that the specific pairing we have postulated immediately suggests a possible copying mechanism for the genetic material."*

That sentence now stands encoded in the very alphabet it announced, on this shelf, in his honor.

Dedicated also to the whole company without whom there is no alphabet to write in:

- **Friedrich Miescher** (1844–1895), who first isolated DNA in 1869, eighty-two years before its shape was known.
- **Rosalind Franklin** (1920–1958) and **Raymond Gosling** (1926–2015), whose Photograph 51 carried the evidence.
- **Maurice Wilkins** (1916–2004) and his colleagues at King's College, London.
- **James D. Watson** (1928–2025), co-author of the 1953 paper.

None of them encoded a book. All of them made every encoding possible.
