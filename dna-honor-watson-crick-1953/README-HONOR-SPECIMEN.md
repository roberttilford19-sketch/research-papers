# In Honor — The Sentence That Announced the Copying Mechanism, Written in DNA

An honor specimen, made 2026-11-14 by Robert Tilford of Hays, Kansas, and given free.

## The sentence

> "It has not escaped our notice that the specific pairing we have postulated immediately suggests a possible copying mechanism for the genetic material."

— James D. Watson and Francis H. C. Crick, "Molecular Structure of Nucleic Acids: A Structure for Deoxyribose Nucleic Acid," *Nature* 171 (4356), pp. 737–738, 25 April 1953. The closing sentence of the paper that announced the double helix — often called the greatest understatement in science. It is quoted here, briefly and with credit, as quotation.
