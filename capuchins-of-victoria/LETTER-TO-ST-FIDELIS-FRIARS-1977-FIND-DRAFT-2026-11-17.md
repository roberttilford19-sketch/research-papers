Dear Father Curtis, and the friars of St. Fidelis,

I am a layman over in Hays, and your basilica has been on my heart for a long while. I spend my days on research — old records, diocesan papers, the province's own histories — and I lately put together the history of the Capuchins in Victoria. One find in it was too good to keep in a drawer, so I am writing to put it in your hands first.

On April 14, 1977, the Province of St. Conrad was solemnly established at St. Fidelis Church in Victoria — two days after its legal division from the Province of St. Augustine on April 12, 1977. The division date stands in the province's own commissioned history; the establishment at Victoria stands in The Chancellor, the Thomas More Prep newspaper in Hays, of January 28, 1977, which also reports why St. Conrad of Parzham was chosen as the new province's patron: for the sake of his promise to Father Hyacinth Epp — the friar who brought the Capuchins to Herzog in 1878, and to Pennsylvania before that. And the province's first headquarters was not Denver. It was Hays, from 1977 to 1980.

Which is to say: St. Fidelis is not an outpost of the province. It is the province's birthplace. And the line still runs through Victoria — the current provincial, Father David Songy, was elected at a chapter held in your church in May 2025.

I am building a small booklet from the full research — the order's founding, the road from Pittsburgh to Herzog, the building of the basilica, and Saint Fidelis himself — and when it is ready I would like to give it to you, freely, for the rectory and for anyone who loves that church. This letter is just the first page of it, because this one fact belonged to you before it belonged to a book.

With gratitude for your prayers and your welcome,

Robert Tilford
Hays, Kansas
