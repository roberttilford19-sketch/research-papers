# The Capuchins of Victoria

Research gathered by Robert Tilford of Hays, Kansas, on the Capuchin friars of St. Fidelis Basilica, Victoria, Kansas — the order's founding, its road to Kansas, the building of the basilica, and the province it gave birth to. Prepared as a gift for the friars of St. Fidelis; shelved here so that every claim in the accompanying letter and booklet can be checked against its source, in the open.

## The 1977 find

On 12 April 1977 the Pennsylvania Province of St. Augustine was divided into two separate and independent provinces (the province's own commissioned *Audit of the Capuchin Province of Mid-America (aka Province of St. Conrad)*, 1 March 2019 — VERIFIED). Two days later, on 14 April 1977, the new Mid-America province — the Province of St. Conrad — was solemnly established at St. Fidelis Church in Victoria, Kansas, and St. Conrad of Parzham was chosen as its patron because of his promise to Fr. Hyacinth Epp, the friar who established the Capuchins in Pennsylvania and Kansas (*The Chancellor*, newspaper of Thomas More Prep, Hays, 28 January 1977, "Friars Make Plans," page 1 — contemporary reporting, graded SUPPORTED). The province's first headquarters was Hays, Kansas (1977–1980). St. Fidelis is not an outpost of the Province of St. Conrad; it is the province's birthplace.
