## What this is — and what it is not

This is an **in-silico encoding printed as data**: the complete text of
Robert Tilford's Tao Te Ching translation master, taken from the source file
of record below, written in the four DNA letters A, C, G, T under the codec
stated below. It is an **encoding, not a translation**: no wording was
translated, modernized, or altered in any way by this lane — the source
file's bytes were converted, exactly as they stand, and decoding returns
them exactly. The source master itself carries its own face — **DRAFT ·
UNREVIEWED**, its Honesty Gate standing open until a collaborator qualified
in Classical Chinese has reviewed the rendering — and this encoding inherits
that face unchanged: encoding a draft does not review it.
**Nothing has been synthesized. No physical DNA exists.** Physical synthesis
is a separate future step that would require funding and a laboratory; no
funding is in place and no physical DNA has been made, and synthesis is not
part of this deposit. No claim of priority or firstness is made anywhere in
this deposit: what is claimed is only what the verification figures below
prove.

## The codec (exactly the house codec)

Text as UTF-8 bytes → binary bits, most significant bit first → every two
bits become one base: **00 = A, 01 = C, 10 = G, 11 = T**. One byte becomes
four bases. The mapping is lossless and reversible: decoding the base stream
returns the byte stream exactly, which the separate decoder's report in
this deposit proves by hash.
